About Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The Heatwave Intelligence System provides heatwave monitoring, weather forecasts and early warning alerts to protect people during any extreme heat conditions.

Data Sources

Weather information is collected from AWS Sensors, IMD Database, satellite observations and weather models.

Stakeholders

The portal helps Citizens, Meteorologists, Disaster Management Officers, Hospitals and Government Authorities.
